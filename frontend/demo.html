🔴 Root Cause
Obs: SP→RJ + SP→BA = 34% of all late deliveries. Late orders jumped from ~0.2K/month (late 2017) to ~1.5K (early 2018) — a +650% surge.
💡 Last-mile capacity at RJ and BA could not scale with marketplace growth. Supply chain bottleneck, not demand issue.
→ Fulfillment deep dive: map delays by stage. Priority carrier contracts at RJ/BA. Consider micro-fulfillment centers.
📊 Scale
Obs: Platform promises 23.8 days on average, actual delivery 12.5 days. Buffer of 11.2 days = massive over-promise.
💡 SLA buffer too large — tightening would improve conversion without ops changes, since actual performance already exceeds the tighter target.
→ Tighten SLA from 23.8 → 18 days for top routes. Test impact on conversion rate vs complaint rate.
✅ Action Priority
Obs: Freight cost varies 2× across states — CE/PE avg R$38–42 vs SP avg R$18. Large items (furniture, computers) add 3–5 delivery days vs avg.
💡 Freight cost concentrated in distant northern states. Category-specific routing could reduce cost and time simultaneously.
→ Proactive notification for orders with >2 day delay risk: expected 2–3pp review score improvement. Category-aware routing for large items.

💳 Payment
Obs: Credit card R$12.54M (81%), AOV 164 — highest. Voucher AOV only 98 — lowest by far. Boleto AOV 145 — important in Brazil.
💡 Voucher drag on margin. Credit card users spend 67% more per order than voucher users.
→ Card-first checkout + installment tuning. Review voucher terms — low-value only? Boleto: faster processing where feasible.
🚚 Freight
Obs: signaling_and_security freight 30.4%, telephony 22.4%, stationery 20.5%. These are high-growth categories being eroded by freight.
💡 Freight ratio above 20% starts to hurt margin meaningfully at scale. These categories need carrier-level intervention.
→ Carrier renegotiation for signaling/telephony/stationery. Target −1.5 to −2pp freight ratio. Packaging optimization.
📈 Growth
Obs: watches_gifts growth 275.6%, small_appliances 6087%. bed_bath_table and health_beauty stable at top. security_and_services at 0% growth.
💡 Emerging categories breaking out. Stagnant categories may need re-strategy or reallocation.
→ Double down on watches_gifts + small_appliances. Investigate security_and_services stagnation — lifecycle, pricing, relevance.

🔗 Delivery–Sentiment Link
Obs: OTIF <88% → review score drops to 3.6–3.8. Each 5pp OTIF drop = −0.4 stars. The correlation is consistent across all months analyzed.
💡 Late delivery is the #1 driver of negative reviews — above product quality. Operational fixes directly translate to sentiment improvement.
→ Fix OTIF first. Each 1pp OTIF improvement ≈ +0.03 avg review score. Improving OTIF 91.9% → 95% = +0.15 avg stars.
📦 Category Pain
Obs: Categories with R$1–1.5M revenue still show 11–15% low-rating share. High revenue ≠ high satisfaction. Core categories carrying quality risk.
💡 Above 12% low-rating share, negative reviews start to materially hurt trust and conversion. Several top categories are near or above this threshold.
→ Quality audit for high-impact high-pain categories. Vendor performance review. Category-specific post-purchase surveys.
✅ Action
Obs: Review volume grew from ~0 (late 2016) to ~7.46K/month (late 2017/early 2018). Persistent share of 1–2★ and 3★ ratings alongside positive growth.
💡 Scale amplifies both positive and negative signals. Low-rating share staying constant while volume grows = structural issue, not noise.
→ Target −3pp low-rating share in top 5 high-pain categories within 2 quarters. Proactive communication for late orders: expected 2–3pp review mix improvement.
